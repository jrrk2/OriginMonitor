OriginMonitor gallery

Deep-sky images taken unattended with a Celestron Origin (152 mm f/2.2 RASA, 1.48″ per pixel) from Cambridge, UK, between 31 December 2025 and 12 November 2026: 23 targets, 37.1 hours of stacked exposure over 16 nights. Each target is one stack of all its good frames from every night (tools/restack.py), shown as the live stacker's own preview at half resolution (background extraction, colour neutralisation and a stretch, no further processing), cropped to the area every rotated frame covers. The Crab is the exception: a full-resolution stack (tools/fullres_stack.py) of 4.1 hours over eight nights, with broadband and quad-band luminance and the quad-band filter's colour; its broadband-to-narrowband crossfade shows what each records. Click a picture for the full field (about 75′ × 50′).

The stacker, scheduler and unattended supervisor are in the OriginMonitor repository; how they drive the telescope is drawn in the state-machine charts.

Photographs © Jonathan Kimmitt. Star HFR is the median half-flux radius of the stacked frames. “Antlia quad-band” is a narrowband filter fitted on 15 October 2026; earlier frames are unfiltered.
